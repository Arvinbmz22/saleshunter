01 / VIDEO A · SURFER ACADEMY

Modern SEO & AI search

Surfer Academy connects the classic crawl–index–rank model to conversational search.

▶ 00:00–05:32 What SEO actually is

The “SEO is dead” myth

AI chatbots, AI search engines and Google AI Overviews have led commentators to declare SEO dead. Yet Google processes billions of searches per day, search volume grew 20% in the last year, and Google sends 190 times more web traffic to websites than ChatGPT. Discovery is bifurcating into traditional search engines and AI-powered assistants—not disappearing.

Definition / تعریف: Search Everywhere OptimizationAshley Liddell’s framework: optimize brand visibility wherever potential customers search for solutions.

The three-step search engine pipeline

01 · CRAWL / خزشBots follow hyperlinks to discover content.
02 · INDEX / نمایه‌سازیDiscovered content is parsed into the search database. If a page is not indexed, it cannot appear.
03 · RANK / رتبه‌بندیThe index is scored for relevance, trustworthiness and utility.

AI search relies on this infrastructure: ChatGPT uses Bing’s index when browsing live web; Perplexity has its own crawler but relies heavily on Bing; independent indexes still crawl and prioritize pages that rank well traditionally.

Business intent over vanity metrics

SEO should produce revenue, leads and paying clients. Social/display advertising is push marketing; search is pull marketing. A query such as “best proposal software for agencies” expresses active purchase intent.

Anchor case studies: PandaDoc (B2B SaaS), Counter Culture Coffee (e-commerce), Huntress (managed cybersecurity).

▶ 05:32–10:00 Search intent & zero-click realities

Informational

Learn: “how to train your puppy.”

Commercial investigation

Evaluate: “best dog training classes.”

Transactional / navigational

Act or reach a brand: “dog training class near me sign up”; “PetSmart dog training.”

Search the target keyword yourself: the top ten organic results show what users want. Head terms have high volume but ambiguous intent—“coffee” has about 650,000 US searches/month. Long-tail queries have lower volume, clearer intent and lower competition, such as “best dark roast coffee beans for espresso.” New sites should build authority on long-tail queries first.

Zero-click reality / واقعیت جست‌وجوی بدون کلیکAI Overviews can satisfy a query without a click. Counter this with original perspective, proprietary experience, downloadable tools or deep procedures that cannot fit in a three-sentence answer.

▶ 10:00–23:45 Keywords, content and authority

Keyword sweet spotQuestion

Funnel examples / نمونه قیفPandaDoc: “contract template” (~300,000/month) → free template download → email capture → nurture → trial/signup. Counter Culture Coffee: educational “pour over coffee” (34,000/month) supports “pour over coffee ratio” (10,000) and “cold brew ratio” (6,800).

Keyword research disciplines: ideate with Google Search Console, Autocomplete, PAA, subreddits, LLM prompts and Keyword Surfer; validate demand, difficulty, fit and intent; cluster semantically related terms under one URL to prevent cannibalization. Prioritize money pages, build one complete cluster, then repeat—do not scatter across topics.

▶ 23:45–30:09 Content that ranks

ResearchAnalyze the top five pages: coverage, omissions, depth, angles and media.
StructureOutline to match intent before drafting.
WriteUse AI for scaffolding, then add experience, contrarian insight, original data, quotes and verifiable cases.
OptimizeUse NLP benchmarks such as Surfer Content Score.
The 96.5% problemAhrefs’ study of 14 billion pages found over 96% receive zero Google traffic. Average content will not rank.

On-page, links, technical and AI-search checklist

  • On-page: title keyword near front and under 60 characters; one H1; logical H2/H3; clean slug; 150–160 character meta description; contextual internal links; descriptive alt text and WebP images.
  • Links: earn editorial links with original data, journalist platforms such as SOS/Featured.com, and calculators/templates built with AI coding assistants. A coffee-to-water calculator targeted KD 8.
  • Technical: CMSs such as WordPress, Webflow and Shopify cover basics; Screaming Frog (free up to 500 URLs) finds 404s, missing tags, redirect chains, orphans and uncompressed images. Claim Google Business Profile and earn/respond to reviews.
  • AI visibility: use clear parsing structure, direct answers, headings and schema; dense topical clusters; “best of” placements; mention-gap analysis. Since GSC cannot show AI-chat visits, track AI mentions and competitor citation share.
02 / VIDEO B · AHREFS

Foundational SEO mechanics

SEO success is disciplined execution of fundamentals that compound—not obscure tricks. Ahrefs reports more than 1M monthly organic visits to its own site.

▶ 00:00–07:54 Fundamentals

Organic search offers free traffic without marginal CPC, stable topic demand compared with social spikes, and scale across more than 4 billion Google users. Google crawls from seed URLs, follows links, indexes hundreds of billions of pages, then evaluates backlinks, intent, depth and context.

▶ 07:54–40:28 Keyword research & the 3 C’s

Traffic Potential (TP) can outperform primary volume because one page can rank for hundreds or thousands of long-tail variations. Score business potential: 3 = indispensable solution; 2 = helps but is not required; 1 = loosely related; 0 = vanity traffic. Assess ranking difficulty and page-level competition.

Content type

Blog, product, category, landing page or video.

Content format

Tutorial, list, opinion, guide or comparison.

Content angle

“For beginners,” “free,” “in 2026,” “fastest.”

Use competitor gap analysis in Ahrefs Keywords Explorer/Site Explorer. KD is based on referring domains to ranking pages. Also inspect DR versus UR, quality, topical authority and intent alignment; a low-authority page with perfect intent can outrank an authoritative mismatch.

▶ 40:28–59:39 On-page mechanics

Put the target keyword early in a compelling title; use one H1 and logical H2/H3 headings; keep the slug short and clean; write a useful meta description for CTR; cover the topic naturally without stuffing; and use descriptive hyphenated image names, alt text and compression.

▶ 59:39–01:43:28 Link equity and outreach

A good backlink combines authority, relevance, descriptive anchor text and editorial in-content placement. Dofollow links pass equity; rel="nofollow", rel="sponsored" and rel="ugc" are generally hints. Build linkable assets, replace broken links, reclaim unlinked brand mentions, and send personalized value-first pitches to relevant editors.

▶ 01:43:28–01:57:03 Technical SEO and auditing

robots.txt

Controls crawl permissions.

XML sitemap

Lists canonical URLs to discover and index.

rel="canonical"

Designates the primary version among duplicates.

Use logical, shallow architecture; improve response time with caching/CDNs such as Cloudflare; and monitor Core Web Vitals. Audit with Ahrefs Webmaster Tools or Site Audit: verify ownership via GSC/DNS, schedule weekly crawls with external link tracking, monitor Health Score, then resolve Errors before Warnings. Watch for 404s, orphan pages, redirect chains (301 → 301 → 200), duplicate descriptions, missing titles and missing alt attributes.

Seven rules
  1. AI search is built on traditional indexation.
  2. Match the format and angle that intent rewards.
  3. Build money pages before scaling information.
  4. Complete one cluster before scattering across topics.
  5. Surpass page one substantially—the quality hurdle is high.
  6. One relevant editorial link beats hundreds of weak links.
  7. Every article should link to cluster resources and money pages.
04 / EXECUTION

Technical reference & checklists

On-page optimization

  • □ Keyword-forward title under 60 characters
  • □ Exactly one H1 and logical H2/H3
  • □ Human-readable keyword slug
  • □ 150–160 character action-oriented meta
  • □ Descriptive filename, alt text, WebP
  • □ 2–5 contextual internal links

Technical diagnostic

  • □ Critical directories not blocked in robots.txt
  • □ XML sitemap submitted, with no 404/redirects
  • □ Correct canonical tags
  • □ Screaming Frog/Ahrefs weekly crawl
  • □ Fix 404s, loops and orphan pages
  • □ Test Core Web Vitals, CDN and caching
Common mistakesAvoid generic AI slop, neglecting money pages, choosing by volume alone, unrelated topic scattering, intent mismatch, and overcomplicating technical SEO before publishing useful content.
05 / APPLICATION

Real-world case studies

01 · PandaDoc

Ranks for “contract template” (~300,000/month), then turns the top-funnel visit into a free download, email lead and onboarding sequence. Its cluster spans legal templates, e-signature and sales workflows.

02 · Counter Culture Coffee

Uses a #1 brewing tutorial for “pour over coffee” (34,000/month), supporting ratio guides (10,000 and 6,800). Links from WikiHow, Gear Patrol and Breville pass authority toward subscription and product pages.

03 · Huntress

Uses a clean hierarchy and slugs such as /topic/ransomware-recovery-guide and /platform/managed-edr. Original live-threat research earns citations from major tech publications, Wikipedia and Google Cloud Threat Intelligence.
